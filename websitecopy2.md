### Where I’d be your weakest hire

Being straight with you, in order:

- **Never recruited at the frontier research bar.** Senior and staff+ engineering in a nasty competitive market, sure. Research scientists where fifty people in the world can do the job is a level up, and I’d be learning that calibration in front of you for a few months
- **I build tools faster than I measure them.** People used the stuff I made and told me it helped. I never actually checked whether it made a decision better. Your thing about efficacy over efficiency is what made that obvious to me, and it’s the gap I’d most want to close here
- **I take too long to commit to a recommendation.** Gather more than I need before I’ll say the thing out loud. Working on getting there at 80% and showing my reasoning instead of waiting to feel certain
